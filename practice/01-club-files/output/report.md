# Organization report

## Categories

- `announcements/`: announcement and its byte-identical copy. Both files remain separate.
- `budget/`: proposed budget draft; its contents say it is not approved.
- `equipment/`: equipment list and backup. Their contents match, so both copies are retained.
- `feedback/`: feedback questions.
- `meetings/`: meeting notes.
- `planning/`: next steps.
- `promotion/`: poster text.
- `proposals/`: v1 and v2 proposals. They differ in activity type and duration; filenames do not establish approval or which is final.
- `contingencies/`: rain plan.

## Duplicate and version observations

`announcement.txt` and `announcement_copy.txt` have identical contents. `equipment_list.txt` and `equipment_backup.txt` have identical contents. All four files are copied individually. `proposal_final.txt` and `proposal_final2.txt` have different contents (outdoor/30 minutes versus indoor/20 minutes); both are preserved and require human discussion.

## Unresolved questions

Which proposal, if either, will the group choose? Are the draft budget and suggested time/location ever approved? This organization does not make those decisions.
